My real issue with AI is cognitive laziness. I fear that someday someone may ask an AI model "What is the answer to the ultimate question of Life, the Universe, and Everything?"[^1] and be perfectly happy with the answer "42"!

[^1]: The famous question posed in [The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hitchhiker%27s_Guide_to_the_Galaxy){:target="_blank"}.
